Tags on a recipe

One field, one face. Every tag looks the same, whether it is a word the app also understands or a word that only means something to you.

Phone frames are 393 points wide, the real logical width of the device, so chip sizes, gaps and type here are 1:1 with what gets built. The control does not exist yet: tags are currently only settable from the library, so this is the whole of what #101 draws.

Suggestions come from tags already in the library and from the coffee vocabulary, in one undifferentiated list. Casing is kept exactly as typed.

The longest word in the vocabulary has to wrap rather than truncate. A tag that is cut is a tag you cannot verify you typed right.

What is deliberately not here

A tag that matches the coffee vocabulary is still recognised: intentTags(recipe) computes it, and #104 will use it to hold a recipe's intent against what was actually brewed. None of that shows in the editor, because until #104 has a comparison to point at, a second typeface would be a difference the user has to guess at.
