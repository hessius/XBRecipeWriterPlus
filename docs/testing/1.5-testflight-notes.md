# XBRW++ 1.5 — what to test, and what changed since 1.0.0

**Build:** 1.5 (2) · iOS · TestFlight
**Previous build testers saw:** 1.0.0 (1), 28 August — the App Store release
**Between them:** 276 commits and three milestones

---

## Read this first

1.0.0 was a card writer. It read an xBloom recipe card over NFC, let you edit
the recipe, and wrote it back. That is all it did.

1.5 still does that, and it has also grown a Bluetooth stack. It connects to the
machine, sends a recipe to it, starts the brew, watches the brew happen, and
keeps a record of it afterwards. The recipe editor gained bypass water, which a
card cannot store at all — it only exists on the Bluetooth path.

**So the important thing about this build is that most of what is new cannot be
tested without the machine.** There is no simulator for a coffee grinder. If you
have an xBloom Studio, sections C through G are where the value is. If you do
not, sections A, B and H still need eyes.

Everything is dark mode only. That is deliberate, not an oversight.

---

## What is new, in one page

**Talking to the machine (new in this build)**
- Connect to an xBloom Studio over Bluetooth, from Settings or the dot in the
  home header.
- BREW on any recipe: the app sends the recipe and starts it, no card needed.
- A live brew screen — a graph of water against time, one rung per stage, and a
  running water / cup / time readout.
- Dismiss the brew and a mini bar keeps it in view; tap it to go back.
- Every brew is kept. Brew history, with the full chart, per-stage detail, and
  export as an image or as JSON.
- A frame log per brew, so an odd brew leaves evidence we can read.

**Bypass water (new, and the least proven thing here)**
- Bypass water is a splash of hot water added straight to the cup at the end,
  bypassing the coffee, to dilute a strong brew without under-extracting it.
- You can now turn it on in the editor, set its volume and temperature, see it
  as its own band on the stage profile, and brew it over Bluetooth.
- The brew screen draws it as a fourth, dashed rung and as a dashed box sitting
  on top of the target line.
- **A card cannot store bypass water.** The editor says so; a card write drops
  it and warns you first.

**Recipes and cards**
- Share a recipe as an xBloom link, back out to anyone.
- Grind size guidance in the editor.
- A card holds ten stages. The app now says so instead of writing a card the
  machine will refuse, and it refuses a write that would run off the end.
- Ratios like 1:15.5 are accepted — xBloom's own shared recipes use them. They
  still cannot be written to a card, which only stores whole numbers.

**The home screen**
- A machine status dot in the header, with a panel of what the machine is doing.
- A BREW shortcut on every recipe row — **in five shapes, and we want you to
  pick one.** Settings → Recipe list → BREW shortcut shape. Four of the five are
  going to be deleted; tell us which one to keep.
- Swipe a recipe row: the two directions now do different things.

## Before you start

- iOS 17 or later, a real device. NFC and Bluetooth both need one.
- For sections C–G: an xBloom Studio, water in the tank, beans in the hopper,
  and a pod or dripper you don't mind sacrificing to a test.
- Grant Bluetooth when asked. If you refuse it, section C is over.
- **Back up first if you care about your recipes:** Settings → Back up my
  recipes. This build changes how recipes are stored on disk more than once.

## A. The things 1.0.0 already did — check we did not break them

- [ ] **A1.** Read a card. Hold a genuine xBloom card to the top of the phone.
      The recipe opens, with its name, dose, ratio, stages and grind size.
- [ ] **A2.** Write that same card straight back, unchanged. It should still
      brew on the machine afterwards. *This is the one irreversible test in the
      list — a malformed write is not trivially recoverable, so use a card you
      can afford to lose.*
- [ ] **A3.** Edit a recipe: change the dose, the ratio, a stage volume, a
      temperature, the grind size. Save. Reopen. Everything stuck.
- [ ] **A4.** Import: paste an xBloom share link, or share one into the app from
      Safari. The recipe arrives with the right numbers.
- [ ] **A5.** Delete a recipe, and restore your backup. Nothing lost.

## B. Recipes, ratios and card limits

- [ ] **B1.** Open a recipe with **eleven or more stages**. The editor should
      say a card holds ten, and that the recipe can still be saved and brewed
      over Bluetooth. It should not offer to write a card that will fail.
- [ ] **B2.** Ten stages exactly: a card write should be offered, and the
      machine should accept the card.
- [ ] **B3.** Import `https://share-h5.xbloom.com/?id=Wr8X9ShLh53zxRv%2FIOdvAw%3D%3D`
      — a **1:15.5** recipe. It should import and save without being nagged to
      fix the water. Half-point ratios are legal in the app now.
- [ ] **B4.** Change a recipe's ratio by hand. The target line on the stage
      graph should move **immediately**, and Auto fix should clear its own
      banner without you having to save, close and reopen.
- [ ] **B5.** Look up a recipe by XID in the editor. Typing should not drop
      characters, and a lookup that finds nothing should say so.

## C. Connecting to the machine

- [ ] **C1.** Settings → the machine card. Scan, pick your Studio, connect.
- [ ] **C2.** The dot in the home header goes live. Tap it: the panel says what
      the machine is doing and how much water it has. REFRESH updates it.
- [ ] **C3.** Kill the app and reopen it. It should reach for the machine it
      remembers, without you asking.
- [ ] **C4.** Walk out of range. The app should say *not in range* rather than
      *not connected* — they are different sentences now and should be the right
      one.
- [ ] **C5.** Background the app during a connection, come back. It gives the
      radio up and takes it back.

## D. Brewing over Bluetooth

- [ ] **D1.** BREW a simple three-stage recipe. Grind, bloom, pours, done.
- [ ] **D2.** The brew screen: the water line tracks the plan, the stage ladder
      scrolls itself to the stage that is running, the water / cup / time
      figures move.
- [ ] **D3.** Swipe the brew sheet down mid-brew. The mini bar appears and keeps
      drawing. Tap it to reopen. Nothing resets.
- [ ] **D4.** Brew with the tank nearly empty. The refusal should be amber, name
      the recipe's own volume, offer TRY AGAIN, and write no history row.
- [ ] **D5.** **A brew must not claim you ran out of water when you did not.**
      This has bitten twice and been fixed twice. If you see "ran out of water"
      on a brew that plainly finished, that is the bug — grab the frame log.
- [ ] **D6.** Let the machine hold on overflow protection. The lane should
      re-scale, the fill and the line should turn amber, and the finished chart
      should show the gap with `+N s`.
- [ ] **D7.** Brew a recipe with **many stages** — twelve or more. The machine
      accepts these over Bluetooth even though a card cannot hold them. The
      ladder should scroll rather than overlap the graph above it or the buttons
      below it.

## E. Bypass water — the newest thing here

- [ ] **E1.** In the editor, tap `+ BYPASS WATER` on the dashed rung under the
      stages. Set a volume. It should seed at the temperature the brew ends on.
- [ ] **E2.** The brew target under the title should read the stage total plus
      `+ N ML BYPASS`, not one merged number.
- [ ] **E3.** The stage profile should draw the bypass as its own band, sitting
      on top of the stages rather than inside them.
- [ ] **E4.** Try to write that recipe to a card. It should **warn you that the
      bypass will be lost** before it writes anything.
- [ ] **E5.** Brew it. Before the bypass fires, its rung should be there,
      dashed and dimmed, showing the volume it is going to add.
- [ ] **E6.** During the drawdown — the machine waits for the dripper to finish
      before it adds the bypass, which can be a minute — the rung should read
      **WAITING, and it must not be amber.** That wait is not a fault.
- [ ] **E7.** When it fires, the rung fills to its volume, and **the last stage
      should read its own number** — `85/85 ml`, not `90/85 ml` with the bypass
      folded into it.
- [ ] **E8.** The summary's WATER figure should be the brew water, with the
      bypass beside it as a small dashed `+5`, not silently added in.
- [ ] **E9.** Open that brew from history later. It should look the same as it
      did live.
- [ ] **E10.** Open a brew recorded **before this build**. It should be
      unchanged — no bypass rung, no badge.
- [ ] **E11.** Brew something with no bypass at all. Also unchanged.

## F. Brew history

- [ ] **F1.** Settings → Brew history, or the header, or after a brew. All three
      ways in should work.
- [ ] **F2.** Open a record: the chart, the stage ladder, and a stage's own
      detail panel when you tap it.
- [ ] **F3.** Export as an image. **Check the PNG is actually legible and not
      blank** — large views have been known to export blank on iOS. Try it on a
      long, many-stage brew especially.
- [ ] **F4.** Export as JSON. The file should open in anything.
- [ ] **F5.** A long ladder should scroll inside the record, and the exported
      image should contain **all** the stages, not a cropped screenful.

## G. Sharing

- [ ] **G1.** Share a recipe as an xBloom link. Open the link you get on another
      device, or in the official app. It should be the same recipe.
- [ ] **G2.** A recipe with bypass water: share it, import it back. The bypass
      should survive the round trip.

## H. The home screen, and a question for you

- [ ] **H1.** Settings → Recipe list → **BREW shortcut shape**. Five shapes:
      EDGE, TAB, CHIP, GLYPH, SWIPE. Live with each for a bit. **Which one
      should we keep?** The other four are being deleted.
- [ ] **H2.** Swipe a recipe row left, and right. They do different things now.
      A swipe should **not** open the recipe — if a swipe in either direction
      opens the editor, that is a bug we thought we had fixed.
- [ ] **H3.** Turn BREW on rows off entirely. The list should go quiet.
- [ ] **H4.** Settings → Show the COFFEE marker, and Dot matrix pour profile.
      Both should do what they say.

## Known limits — please do not report these

- **Dark mode only.** There is no light mode and there will not be one.
- **Bypass water cannot be written to a card.** The card format has no room for
  it. This is a limitation of xBloom's cards, not of the app.
- **A card holds ten stages.** Also the card format. Bluetooth has no such
  limit.
- **Ratios on a card are whole numbers.** 1:15.5 works in the app and over
  Bluetooth, but a card cannot store the half.
- **No Lock Screen / Dynamic Island live activity yet.** It is planned.
- **Only genuine xBloom cards can be written.** Each card carries a signature
  derived from its serial that the app cannot recompute — it reads it off the
  card and puts it back. Blank NFC tags will not work.
- The machine console under Settings is a developer tool. It is deliberately
  raw.

## If something goes wrong

The useful thing is the **frame log** — the app keeps one per brew, so a brew
that misbehaved has already recorded what the machine actually said.

1. Open the brew in Brew history.
2. Export it as JSON, or copy the frame log.
3. Send that along with what you saw and what you expected.

A screenshot plus the frame log is worth more than a long description. Without
the log, a Bluetooth bug is usually not diagnosable.
