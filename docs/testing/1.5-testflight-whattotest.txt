XBRW++ 1.5 — the first build since 1.0.0, and a much bigger app.

1.0.0 was a card writer: read an xBloom card over NFC, edit, write it back.
1.5 also talks to the machine over Bluetooth. It sends a recipe, starts the
brew, draws it while it happens, and keeps a record afterwards.

Most of what is new needs the hardware. If you have an xBloom Studio, points
3-6 are where the value is. If not, 1, 2 and 7 still need eyes.

WHAT'S NEW
• Brew over Bluetooth — BREW on any recipe, no card needed
• A live brew screen: water against time, a rung per stage, running figures
• A mini bar that keeps the brew in view when you dismiss it
• Brew history, with per-stage detail and export as image or JSON
• Bypass water — extra water straight to the cup, editable and brewable
• Share a recipe as an xBloom link
• Grind size guidance
• A machine status dot in the home header
• Card limits made honest: ten stages, and no write that overruns

PLEASE CHECK

1. Nothing old broke. Read a card, edit a recipe, write the card back, import a
share link, back up and restore. Use a card you can afford to lose — a bad
write is not easily undone.

2. Ratios and card limits. A recipe with 11+ stages should say a card holds ten
and offer Bluetooth instead. Import
https://share-h5.xbloom.com/?id=Wr8X9ShLh53zxRv%2FIOdvAw%3D%3D — a 1:15.5
recipe — and it should just work. Change a ratio: the target line should move
straight away and Auto fix should clear its own banner.

3. Connecting. Connect from Settings. The header dot goes live; its panel shows
state and water. Restart the app — it should reconnect on its own. Out of
range should say "not in range", not "not connected".

4. Brewing. Brew a three-stage recipe. The line should track the plan and the
ladder should scroll to the live stage. Dismiss mid-brew: the mini bar keeps
drawing. Try a low tank (should refuse cleanly, no history row) and a
twelve-stage recipe (the ladder should scroll, not overlap).
IMPORTANT: if a brew that finished normally claims you ran out of water, that
is a bug we have fixed twice. Send the frame log.

5. Bypass water — newest and least proven. Turn it on in the editor: the target
should read the stage total plus "+ N ML BYPASS", and the profile should draw
it as its own band. A card write must warn you it will be dropped. Then brew
it and watch:
 • before it fires: a dashed, dimmed rung showing the volume
 • during the drawdown wait (up to a minute): WAITING, and NOT amber
 • after: the last stage reads 85/85, not 90/85
 • the summary reads brew water with a dashed "+5" beside it
Open it again from history — it should match. Open an older brew — unchanged.

6. History and export. Export a brew as an image: check the PNG is legible and
not blank, especially on a long brew, and that every stage is in it.

7. A question, not a test: Settings → Recipe list → BREW shortcut shape offers
five shapes (EDGE, TAB, CHIP, GLYPH, SWIPE). Four are going to be deleted.
Live with them and tell us which to keep. Also: swiping a row should never
open the recipe.

KNOWN AND DELIBERATE
Dark mode only. Bypass water and 11+ stages cannot be stored on a card — that
is xBloom's card format, not us. Card ratios are whole numbers. Only genuine
xBloom cards can be written; blank tags cannot. No Lock Screen live activity
yet. The machine console in Settings is a raw developer tool.

REPORTING
The app keeps a frame log per brew. Open the brew in history, export it, and
send that with a screenshot. A Bluetooth bug without the log usually cannot be
diagnosed.
